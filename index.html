02 — ABOUT

About
Dan.

I am Dan Elsaigh (Abdelrahman Mohamed), a UI/UX and brand identity designer working out of Alexandria, Egypt. Over three years of focused freelance practice alongside studies at the Faculty of Fine Arts, Alexandria University, I have developed visual identities, packaging systems, and digital product experiences for growing brands.

I approach design with construction logic and structural clarity — from a packaging dieline that must fold and assemble cleanly in production, to a responsive brand mark that remains legible at a 16px favicon, to a digital interface that feels effortless from the very first tap. Systems over superficial decoration, always engineered with purpose.
