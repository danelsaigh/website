02 — ABOUT

About
Dan.

I'm Dan Elsaigh, a UI/UX and brand designer working out of Alexandria, Egypt, studying at the Faculty of Fine Arts, Alexandria University. For the past three years I've worked freelance across brand identity, packaging systems, and digital product design.

I like problems with a construction spec attached — a dieline that has to fold correctly, a logo that has to survive a 16px favicon, an interface that has to hold up past the first click. Systems over decoration, always with a point of view.
